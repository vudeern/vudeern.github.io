Where I’ve worked, and the problems I took on.

Aug 2025 – Present

The Stachowiak Lab

Undergraduate Research Assistant

A microscopy movie can have thousands of frames. I built a Python workflow to carry that movie through Fiji and Filopodyan, cutting the time spent analyzing a batch by 90%.

The work extends past launching the analysis. The pipeline checks whether files are usable, records where a run failed, and packages measurements and images into reports. Those details matter when a long batch finishes and you need to know what actually worked.

I checked the resulting measurements against prior work using MATLAB statistical analysis. That comparison found no statistically significant differences.

Jan – Aug 2025

The Neptune Lab

Undergraduate Research Assistant

I worked with 80 walking trials from 20 participants, turning motion-capture and force-plate data into force profiles that could be compared.

The comparison was between forward and backward walking. I used Vicon to quantify ground and joint reaction forces and examine how the profiles changed with walking direction.
